Compliance guideSix Chilean rules every AI agent must respect when selling.

Tax and consumer-protection rules that Comercio IA applies, each with its official source from the SII, SERNAC or the law.

This guide summarises tax and consumer-protection rules relevant to implementing Comercio IA in Chile, reviewed as of 15 October 2026. Their application depends on the operation and on each participant's role. It does not replace the analysis of a specific implementation.

01. A boleta reaches the SII within 1 hour

Each electronic boleta must be sent to the SII immediately, within at most 1 hour of being generated. The clock runs from generation (signature and timbre), not from the accounting date. The sending deadline does not determine the legal moment of issuance. Facturas and other documents follow a different rule: they are sent before delivery to the receiver or before goods are moved.

What it means for an AI sale
The store issues the boleta when appropriate for the operation and sends it automatically to the SII within that deadline, with retries and alerts.
In the standard
tax_documents[].issued_at, sii_track_id, sii_status

Sources

  • SII Res. Ex. No. 74 of 2020, item F.6
  • SII FAQ 001.380.7671

02. Generic RUT 66.666.666-6, except above 135 UF

The generic RUT 66.666.666-6 is used in the cases the SII format allows, for non-periodic, non-residential sales or services when the RUT is unknown and no applicable internal identifier exists. It is not a universal rule for every boleta.

For sales covered by SII Resolution No. 44 of 2025 whose value exceeds the 135 UF threshold, the payer must be identified by name and RUT, the payment method recorded and the goods described. The payer may be different from the buyer. The threshold in pesos is set each year using the UF value of 31 December of the previous year; it is not recalculated with the UF of the purchase date. Phone and email are additional, not required by this rule. The implementation must collect and tell apart these data before issuing the document.

What it means for an AI sale
For large purchases the agent must ask for the buyer's details before payment; if they are missing, the store does not issue with the generic RUT.
In the standard
tax_document.receiver (see the 2026-10-16 proposal)

Sources

  • Tax Code art. 92 ter; SII Res. Ex. No. 44 of 2025
  • SII Electronic Boleta Format v4.2

03. No daily boleta report since 2022

The daily sales summary (formerly the folio consumption report) was eliminated for all issuers on 1 August 2022. The SII builds the Sales Register each day from the boletas it receives. The issuer must still send each boleta on time, review the register when needed and file the monthly F29.

What it means for an AI sale
An implementation doesn't need to produce daily summaries; it must make sure every boleta reaches the SII.
In the standard
tax_documents[].sii_status

Sources

  • SII Res. Ex. No. 53 of 2022
  • SII FAQ 001.380.7679

04. Factura: the buyer accepts or claims within 8 days

For an electronic factura, the eight-calendar-day period to accept or claim is counted from its receipt by the SII, under the applicable regime. Distinguish acceptance of the factura from the acknowledgement of receipt of goods or services. Facturas reported as paid in cash are treated differently and do not enable the same claim procedure in the register.

The general payment period is 30 calendar days from the buyer's receipt of the factura, subject to legal exceptions and conditions. References to a purchase order, HES or contract depend on the operation and the applicable regime; it must not be stated that they are always optional. An agent may support authorized actions, but it does not replace the buyer's authorization and cannot declare a receipt that did not happen.

What it means for an AI sale
The acknowledgement comes from the buying company in the SII, not from its purchasing agent. Letting the agent request references avoids commercial rejections.
In the standard
tax_document.receiver; references in the 2026-10-16 proposal

Sources

  • Ley 19.983 arts. 2, 3, 4 and 9
  • SII Circular No. 4 of 2017
  • SII DTE Format, reference section
  • SII FAQ 001.003.7004

05. When an AI sells, the consumer must know

In SERNAC's interpretation (Res. Ex. 33/2022), the consumer must be told, transparently and in time, that they are interacting with artificial intelligence, what it is used for and what data it processes, and be given reasonable grounds when the AI refuses a purchase. SERNAC also warns against dark patterns and manipulation, and notes that personalised prices may amount to arbitrary discrimination.

SERNAC Resolution No. 33 of 2022 contains an interpretive criterion of consumer regulation. The notice about AI use must be visible to the buyer; recording it internally is not enough.

What it means for an AI sale
The agent declares that it is an AI and the store shows that notice to the buyer and keeps it with the sale.
In the standard
consumer_terms.ai_disclosure

Sources

  • SERNAC Interpretive Circular (Res. Ex. No. 33 of 2022)

06. For goods, withdrawal can be excluded in only four cases, disclosed next to the price

In distance sales, the right of withdrawal for goods can be excluded only in the legal categories and conditions: goods that can no longer be marketed as offered because of use or installation beyond inspection; goods that may deteriorate or expire within less than ten days of receipt; goods actually customized; and sealed personal-use or hygiene goods whose opening creates a health or hygiene risk. Check the conditions of DS No. 52 for each product. A customization that was not carried out does not justify the exclusion.

The exclusion must be disclosed in Spanish, before contracting and paying, next to the price and in a size no smaller, using the words “derecho a retracto”. Prior-information requirements also apply to services; if the right is not validly excluded, withdrawal operates within ten days of contracting and before the service is provided. The start of withdrawal is communicated through the means used to contract or the one freely chosen by the consumer, under the regulation. The exclusion does not remove the legal warranty.

What it means for an AI sale
The exclusion notice travels as a mandatory message the agent cannot hide, next to the total.
In the standard
cl.comercioia.policy.retracto (excluded, exclusion_reason) and disclosure messages

Sources

  • DS No. 52 of 2022, Ministry of Economy (Diario Oficial 27-08-2024)
  • SERNAC Res. Ex. No. 272 of 2025
