Comercio IA specification

A Chilean extension to the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) and the Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP). It defines how an AI agent and a store exchange the information Chilean law requires to close a distance sale, and how to pay with Chilean payment methods.

This specification describes tax and consumer-protection rules, but it is not legal advice. Each store is responsible for deciding which notices apply to it.

1. Conventions

  • MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD and MAY are used as in RFC 2119.
  • Amounts are integer Chilean pesos. The peso has no minor unit, so the minor unit is the peso, as in the standards.
  • RUT (Chilean taxpayer ID) without dots, with a hyphen and the modulo 11 check digit, e.g. 76123456-0. Anyone receiving a RUT MUST validate the check digit.
  • Dates in RFC 3339 with a time zone, e.g. 2026-10-19T14:22:05-03:00.
  • Open objects. As UCP asks, the schemas don't close objects or use closed lists: codes are documented as examples, and an unknown value MUST be handled as each field says.
  • Domain authority. Every cl.comercioia.* schema is served from comercioia.cl, with no redirects and no CDN under another name. A schema fetched from another domain is ignored by agents.

2. Declaring it in the store profile

The store declares the extensions under capabilities and the payment methods under payment_handlers in its /.well-known/ucp profile. Agents negotiate by name and version: an extension the agent doesn't announce stays inactive, and checkout continues on the core fields.

The payment receipt as the document

Under art. 54 of the Ley del IVA (DL 825) and SII Res. Ex. 176/2020, the receipt of an electronic payment has "el valor de boleta de ventas y servicios para todos los efectos legales". The resolution's terminal definition includes internet sales. Each taxpayer declares one of two emission models to the SII, and the business states its own in issuance_model, inside the config of its tax_document entry in the profile (new in 2026-10-30; open vocabulary, the codes are examples):

  • payment_receipt: the receipt replaces the boleta for electronic payments. The order carries payment_receipt and no tax_documents[] entry for that sale.
  • always_boleta: the boleta is the document for every sale, even when the business receives an electronic payment.
  • The declaration is made by the business and is not verified against the SII. A receiver MUST NOT assume a boleta when the business has not declared one.
  • Cash sales and bank transfers between accounts need a boleta even at a payment_receipt business.
  • Issuing both documents for one sale duplicates the Registro de Ventas.
  • The receipt is not a DTE and has no folio. In the Registro de Ventas it is a line with SII code 48, which is not a DTE type.
  • Whether a sale above 135 UF (SII Res. Ex. 44/2025) at a payment_receipt business must carry a boleta is left open by this version. The standard does not answer it.
  • A payment receipt is expressed on the order as payment_receipt: operator (for example mercadopago), authorization_code, legend ("Válido como Boleta") and amount_clp, in integer pesos with VAT included. An implementation that cannot capture these data does not fill the field.

Boletas above 135 UF

For sales covered by SII Resolution No. 44 of 2025 whose value exceeds the 135 UF threshold, the boleta must identify the payer by name and RUT, record the payment method and describe the goods (Tax Code art. 92 ter). The payer may be different from the buyer. The threshold in pesos is set each year using the UF value of 31 December of the previous year, not the UF of the purchase date; for 2025 it was CLP 5,186,253. More context in the compliance guide.

  • When the total exceeds 135 UF, the agent MUST collect the RUT and name of the person who pays before payment and send them in tax_document.receiver.
  • If they are missing, the store MUST NOT issue with the generic RUT or complete the checkout. It responds with an error message with code: "cl.comercioia.tax_document.receiver_required" and path: "$.tax_document.receiver". The severity is recoverable if the agent negotiated this extension and can send the data, or requires_buyer_input, with a continue_url where the buyer enters it, if it did not.
  • The store prints the payment method according to the instrument actually used, for example 2 for a card (Webpay Plus, Oneclick Mall, Getnet) and 3 for a transfer (Khipu). With Mercado Pago it depends on the instrument the provider reports.
  • The name of each product in line_items[] goes onto the boleta (NmbItem), so it MUST describe the goods clearly.

Sales to companies

A factura (33 or 34) is for VAT-registered buyers: the agent sends document_choice: "factura" and a receiver with the company's RUT, legal name, line of business, address and comuna. Issuing a factura does not by itself decide whether the Consumer Law applies or is excluded. The buyer's status as final recipient must be reviewed and, where relevant, the protection that Ley 20.416 extends to micro and small businesses.

References to a purchase order, service entry sheet (HES, hoja de entrada de servicios) or contract depend on the operation and the applicable regime; large buyers and the State often reject a factura that does not include them. The agent asks for them in tax_document.references[]; each one is a line of the DTE reference section:

FieldIn the DTERule
typeTpoDocRef (≤3)Document type code, as a string. A numeric one MUST be on the DTE format list: 801 purchase order, 802 order note (nota de pedido), 803 contract, 52 electronic dispatch guide or 33 electronic factura, among others. An alphabetic one, up to 3 letters, is not validated by the SII and covers documents outside that list, such as HES.
numberFolioRef (≤18)The document number exactly as the buyer issued it, with no added prefixes or characters. It can be alphanumeric only for non-tax documents (800 codes or alphabetic types); the folio of a tax document, such as a dispatch guide, is numeric.
dateFchRefDate of the referenced document (YYYY-MM-DD). Mandatory on a factura; the boleta format has no reference date.
reasonRazonRef (≤90)Free text, optional.

HES is not an SII code. The DTE format accepts any alphabetic code without validating it, and each buyer decides how it wants its HES: some ask for HES as the type, others for the HES number under 801 or 802. The store MUST use the code the buyer asked for, unchanged.

Credit payment terms, for example net 30, use the UCP core extension dev.ucp.common.payment.terms, with no fields of our own.

5. Right of withdrawal and legal warranty

Both are policy types inside core policies[], which UCP lets anyone define under their own domain. Like every UCP policy they carry type and description, an object with plain, markdown or html. Their schemas live in consumer_terms.

cl.comercioia.policy.retracto

FieldDefault and rule
window_days10 calendar days from receipt of the goods; for services, within ten days of contracting and before the service is provided.
window_days_without_confirmationUp to 90, if the art. 12 A written confirmation wasn't sent.
refund_within_days45, with no deductions.
excluded, exclusion_reasonIn distance sales, the right of withdrawal for goods can be excluded only in the legal categories and conditions: goods that can no longer be marketed as offered because of use or installation beyond inspection; goods that may deteriorate or expire within less than ten days of receipt; goods actually customized; and sealed personal-use or hygiene goods whose opening creates a health or hygiene risk. Check the conditions of DS No. 52 for each product. A customization that was not carried out does not justify the exclusion. Prior-information requirements also apply to services; if the right is not validly excluded, withdrawal operates within ten days of contracting and before the service is provided. Example codes: not_returnable, perishable, made_to_order, hygiene_opened, service_excluded_by_seller.

Source: Ley 19.496 art. 3 bis b), as amended by Ley 21.398. The exclusion must be disclosed in Spanish, before contracting and paying, next to the price and in a size no smaller, using the words “derecho a retracto”. The exclusion does not remove the legal warranty.

Notices that arrive even when the agent doesn't know the extension

The store MUST also send each withdrawal and warranty notice as a core warning in messages[], with presentation: "disclosure", a code equal to the policy type and a path pointing to it. UCP requires platforms not to hide, collapse or auto-dismiss these notices, and to send the buyer to continue_url if they can't show them. So the legal notices reach the buyer even through an agent that knows nothing about Chile.

6. Credit notes cl.comercioia.shopping.credit_note

Extends dev.ucp.shopping.order. Each core adjustments[] entry (refund, return, withdrawal, cancellation) MAY carry a tax_document with the credit note (61) or debit note (56) issued.

FieldRule
type, folio, issued_at, totalThe document issued, in integer pesos.
ref_type, ref_folio, ref_dateThe original document it refers to (for example, boleta 39).
cod_ref1 cancels the document, 2 corrects text, 3 corrects amounts.
razonReason printed on the document (≤90).

A sale documented by a payment receipt is refunded by annulling the payment through the payment terminal. A credit note identifying the receipt is only the fallback (SII Res. Ex. 176/2020). The credit_note schema does not change in 2026-10-30.

The tax effects of a credit note depend on its cause. For returns or mutual terminations, the six-month period for the adjustments provided in arts. 21 and 70 of DL 825 applies. It is not a general rule for every credit note: error corrections and exempt operations follow different rules. Issuing the document and reducing the VAT debit must be validated separately.

7. Payment methods

The redirect payment methods (Webpay Plus, Mercado Pago, Getnet and Khipu) follow the same pattern, which in UCP is the core escalation pattern. Oneclick Mall (proposed) works differently and is described separately, below:

  1. The store creates the payment with its own account at the provider.
  2. The checkout answers status: "requires_escalation" with a continue_url and a message with severity: "requires_buyer_input".
  3. The buyer pays on the provider's page.
  4. The store confirms the result directly with the provider and creates the order.

The agent never receives card data or credentials. Public configuration (config) MUST NOT include keys or secrets. These payment methods are published by the Comercio IA initiative until each provider publishes its own; every provider is invited to review, co-sign or take theirs over. These are integrations proposed by the initiative: their appearance here does not mean the provider has reviewed or endorsed them, or that they are available on its platform.

Publishing this specification is not, by itself, operating a financial service. In an implementation, the classification depends on the functions actually performed, including payment processing or initiation; it is not settled only by not holding funds. Redirect flows and Oneclick must be described separately, with the authorizations that apply.

Webpay Plus · cl.comercioia.webpay_plus

Provider
Transbank
Create
POST /rswebpaytransaction/api/webpay/v1.2/transactions with the store's commerce code. buy_order ≤26 characters, session_id ≤61, amount in integer pesos, return_url ≤256.
How the buyer pays
Webpay requires a form POST of token_ws, so continue_url points to a store page that submits it automatically.
Confirmation
Commit (PUT …/transactions/{token}) when the buyer returns. Approved only if response_code = 0 and status = AUTHORIZED.
Expiry
5-minute token; the form allows 4 minutes in production (token_ttl_seconds, payment_window_seconds).
Design for
There is no webhook: the store MUST sweep abandoned payments by status query (status can be queried for 7 days). There is no idempotency key: the store uses its own.
Refunds
Reversal or nullification, full or partial, within Transbank's time limits.

Oneclick Mall · cl.comercioia.oneclick_mall (proposed)

Provider
Transbank
Create
A one-time card enrollment on Transbank's page. After that, server-to-server charges with the enrollment's tbk_user and the store's commerce code inside the Mall.
How the buyer pays
Only once, at enrollment. With no active enrollment, the response sets enrollment_required and sends the buyer to continue_url.
Confirmation
Synchronous authorization response.
Design for
The closest thing to in-chat payment available in Chile today. Daily per-user limits are set at affiliation. Before use, confirm with Transbank whether a charge initiated through an AI agent fits its terms.

Getnet · cl.comercioia.getnet

Provider
Getnet (Web Checkout on the PlacetoPay platform)
Create
POST /api/session with the store's credentials; reference ≤32. Sessions last 30 minutes by default (session_ttl_seconds).
How the buyer pays

Khipu · cl.comercioia.khipu

Provider
Khipu (bank transfer)
Create
POST https://payment-api.khipu.com/v3/payments with the store's API key.
How the buyer pays
From their bank account, at the returned payment_url.
Confirmation
Signed webhook (x-khipu-signature), retried by Khipu, plus status query (pending, verifying, done).
Design for
No idempotency key is documented. Payment initiation is subject to Ley 21.521 and CMF regulation, on the phased calendar that applies to each participant and service. An implementer of this specification only creates the charge as the payee and hands the buyer off to Khipu, never capturing the buyer's bank credentials.

11. Versioning and governance

  • Every version is dated (YYYY-MM-DD) and stays published at its own address. A published version is never modified: changes ship in a new version. Version 2026-10-15 corrects statements in 2026-10-09 after a legal review; the earlier version stays published at its own addresses.
  • Third-party extensions version independently of the core, as UCP and ACP allow. Each version states which core versions it requires.
  • Compatibility is kept with the current UCP release and the previous one.
  • Comments are open until 30 November 2026 in the public repository. The first stable release is planned for January 2027, once the path to 1.0 is complete.
  • If a provider publishes an official payment handler, this specification adopts it and marks its own as deprecated.
  • Once adoption is broad, pieces useful to other countries will be proposed to the UCP or ACP core through their formal processes.

12. Aspects that depend on each implementation

Before operating, each implementation must define who sells and who operates the platform under DS 6/2021; what authorization lets the agent act for the buyer; what data each participant shares and under what role and basis; and whether it carries out regulated payment activities. The purchase authorization must define the operation and does not replace the data authorization when one is needed. These obligations are not automatically attributed to the publisher of the specification.
